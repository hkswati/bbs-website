Design is Astral.sh-inspired but adapted for a professional business consultancy. Chosen palette is Option 1 — Gold & Charcoal.

- Background: #F7F5EF (warm off-white)
- Text: #1C1B2E (deep charcoal)
- Gold accent: #C9A84C (matches company logo)
- Dark sections: #2E2D42
- Muted text: #9A8C6A
- Fonts: Outfit (headings, 800–900 weight), Inter (body)
- Decorative motif: gold staircase pixel pattern (like Astral but softer)

**Why:** Company logo is golden; user did not want to strictly follow brand colors beyond that.

**How to apply:** All tokens are in CSS custom properties at top of `themes/beyondborders/static/css/main.css`. Change there to update globally.
