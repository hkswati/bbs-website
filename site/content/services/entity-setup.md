## Setting up a company in Saudi Arabia: structure, timeline and cost

Company formation starts with three questions: which structure fits your plans, how long it will take, and what it will cost. We answer all three in the first consultation, based on your business activity, ownership and hiring plans.

For a standard LLC, the process typically takes around **30 days** once all required documentation is in place. Complex structures or regulated sectors may take longer. Costs depend on the structure, the business activity and the number of people you plan to bring in; we give you a written estimate of timeline and fees before any work starts.

## Branch office or LLC: choosing the right structure

- **Branch office** — an extension of your parent company. It can only carry out the activities the parent company already performs, and the parent remains responsible for its obligations. Often the quicker route when you are serving an existing contract.
- **LLC (limited liability company)** — a separate Saudi legal entity with its own capital and limited liability. The usual choice for companies building a long-term presence and local team.
- **Joint venture or representative office** — for partnerships with a Saudi company, or for market research and promotion without commercial trading.

Choosing the wrong structure at the outset can result in costly restructuring, tax inefficiency, or restrictions on the activities your business can carry out. Our advisors assess your business model, ownership and long-term goals before recommending one.

## Fully foreign-owned companies in Saudi Arabia

Many business activities in Saudi Arabia are open to 100% foreign ownership, without a local partner. Whether yours is depends on the activity and sector; we check this early, explain any conditions such as capital requirements, and guide you through the Ministry of Investment (MISA) process for foreign-owned entities.
