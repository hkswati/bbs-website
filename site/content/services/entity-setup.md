Establishing a legal entity in Saudi Arabia or across the GCC is the foundation of any successful market entry. Beyond Borders guides you through each step of the process, so you can focus on building your business rather than navigating unfamiliar procedures.

## How We Help

- Structure and ownership advice — choosing the most appropriate legal structure (LLC, branch office, joint venture, representative office)
- Saudi market entry advisory, including guidance on the Ministry of Investment (MISA) process for foreign-owned entities
- Guidance on the Ministry of Commerce process
- Guidance on Chamber of Commerce membership
- Guidance on VAT and Zakat requirements
- Regulatory guidance on municipal and sector-specific requirements
- Bank account opening support

## Why It Matters

Choosing the wrong entity structure at the outset can result in costly restructuring, tax inefficiency, or restrictions on the activities your business can legally carry out. Our advisors assess your business model, ownership structure, and long-term goals before recommending a solution.

## Timeline

For a standard LLC, the process typically takes around **30 days** once all required documentation is in place. Complex structures or regulated sectors may take longer — we will give you a realistic estimate during our initial consultation.
