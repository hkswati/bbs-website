Establishing a legal entity in Saudi Arabia or across the GCC is the foundation of any successful market entry. Beyond Borders manages every step so you can focus on building your business, not navigating bureaucracy.

## What We Handle

- Selection of the most appropriate legal structure (LLC, branch office, joint venture, representative office)
- Ministry of Commerce registration and commercial licence
- MISA (Ministry of Investment) licence for foreign-owned entities
- Chamber of Commerce membership
- Tax registration (VAT and Zakat)
- Municipal licences and sector-specific permits
- Bank account opening support

## Why It Matters

Choosing the wrong entity structure at the outset can result in costly restructuring, tax inefficiency, or restrictions on the activities your business can legally carry out. Our advisors assess your business model, ownership structure, and long-term goals before recommending a solution.

## Timeline

Most standard LLC formations are completed within **30 days** of receiving all required documentation. Complex structures or regulated sectors may take longer — we will give you an accurate estimate during our initial consultation.
