Saudi Arabia's development under Vision 2030 has created sustained demand for construction, fit-out, and facilities management across commercial, industrial, and hospitality sectors. Beyond Borders connects businesses with experienced contractors and manages the end-to-end process — ensuring quality, compliance, and on-time delivery.

## What We Offer

- New build and fit-out project management
- Office and commercial space renovation
- Ongoing facilities and maintenance contracts
- Equipment procurement and rental
- Contractor sourcing, vetting, and supervision
- Project cost oversight and progress reporting

## How We Work

We begin by understanding your requirements in full — scope, budget, timeline, and any sector-specific needs. We then identify the right contractors from our established network, oversee the work, and manage the relationship throughout. You have a single accountable point of contact rather than dealing with multiple parties directly.

## Operating in Saudi Arabia

Construction and facilities projects in the Kingdom involve compliance with Saudi Building Code standards, municipality approvals, and coordination with utility authorities. Our local team navigates these requirements as standard, keeping your project moving without administrative delays.
