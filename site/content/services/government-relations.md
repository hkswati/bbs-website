Operating in Saudi Arabia means engaging regularly with a network of ministries, municipalities, and regulatory authorities. Knowing the right procedures, contacts, and timelines is the difference between a business that moves fast and one that stalls in bureaucracy. Our Government Relations team brings that knowledge from day one.

## Services We Provide

- GRO (Government Relations Officer) management and outsourcing
- Commercial licence applications, renewals, and amendments
- Ministry of Commerce (MoC) filings and shareholder updates
- Municipality approvals and zoning permits
- GOSI (General Organization for Social Insurance) registration and compliance
- Absher and Qiwa platform management
- Sector-specific regulatory approvals (SAMA, SFDA, CMA, and others)
- Contract and tender support for government-facing entities
- Compliance audits and company health checks

## How It Works

We assign a dedicated GRO who acts as the operational face of your business with government bodies. They manage all in-person submissions, follow up on pending approvals, and flag compliance gaps before they become problems. You get regular updates without needing to engage the bureaucracy directly.

## Vision 2030 Context

Saudi Arabia's regulatory environment is evolving rapidly as part of Vision 2030 reform programmes. New licencing categories, digital government portals, and streamlined foreign investment rules create real opportunities — but also new compliance obligations that change frequently. We stay current so you can focus on your business.
