Operating in Saudi Arabia means engaging regularly with a network of ministries, municipalities, and regulatory authorities. Knowing the right procedures, platforms, and timelines is the difference between a business that moves fast and one that stalls. Our Government Relations team brings that knowledge from day one, as an ongoing PRO partner rather than a one-off service.

## What Ongoing PRO Support Covers

- GRO (Government Relations Officer) management and outsourcing
- Qiwa & Muqeem support and workforce compliance support
- Absher business account management
- Compliance management — tracking deadlines for your company's records and preparing the paperwork for changes
- Guidance on Ministry of Commerce (MoC) filings and shareholder updates
- Support with municipality and zoning requirements
- GOSI (General Organization for Social Insurance) compliance support
- Regulatory guidance on sector-specific requirements (SAMA, SFDA, CMA, and others)
- Contract and tender support for government-facing entities
- Compliance audits and company health checks

## How It Works: One Dedicated Account Manager

You get a dedicated account manager, backed by our Riyadh-based team, who coordinates your company's government touchpoints. They prepare and track submissions, follow up on pending items, and flag compliance gaps before they become problems. You get regular updates and a single point of contact, without your own team having to manage each process.

## Vision 2030 Context

Saudi Arabia's regulatory environment is evolving rapidly as part of Vision 2030 reform programmes. New licencing categories, digital government portals, and updated foreign investment rules create real opportunities — but also new compliance obligations that change frequently. We stay current so you can focus on your business.
