Running a business in a new market means dealing with a host of administrative and operational tasks that consume time and resources. Our BPO service lets you hand those off to specialists.

## Services We Provide

- Accounting and bookkeeping (IFRS and SOCPA compliant)
- Payroll processing and WPS compliance
- Accounts payable and receivable management
- Month-end and year-end financial reporting
- VAT filing and Zakat returns
- HR administration and employee records management
- Procurement and vendor management support

## The Benefit

Outsourcing to Beyond Borders gives you access to experienced professionals without the overhead of hiring, training, and retaining a full in-house team. You scale the service up or down as your business needs change.
