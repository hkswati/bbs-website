Hiring Saudi nationals is both a regulatory requirement under the Nitaqat programme and, when done well, a genuine business advantage. Our Training Center is designed to equip Saudi employees with the practical skills and professional competencies they need to contribute effectively — helping you build a capable local workforce while maintaining your Saudisation compliance.

## Training Areas

- Sales, business development, and client management
- Finance, accounting, and reporting fundamentals
- Marketing and digital communications
- Operations, logistics, and supply chain basics
- Workplace communication and professional effectiveness
- Role-specific onboarding programmes tailored to your business

## How It Works

We work with your HR team to assess the skills gap for each role, then design a customised training programme delivered at our facility or on-site at your premises. Our trainers combine professional expertise with direct experience in the Saudi business environment — so the content is directly applicable, not generic.

## Vision 2030 Alignment

Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 places the development of Saudi national talent at the centre of economic transformation. Businesses that invest seriously in Saudi workforce development build stronger government relationships, improve their Nitaqat standing, and contribute to a labour market that increasingly delivers the skills the private sector needs.
