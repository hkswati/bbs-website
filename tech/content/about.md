Beyond Borders Technology is the IT division of Beyond Borders Business Consultancy, serving small and medium businesses across Riyadh and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

We are a hands-on technical team covering networking, security, telephony, physical security, automation, and productivity software. Our focus is on businesses that need IT to work reliably — without the overhead of building an in-house IT department.

## How we work

We handle projects from scoping through to delivery and stay available afterwards. Whether it's a new office network, a phone system, CCTV installation, or a Microsoft 365 migration — the same team manages it from start to finish.

For ongoing support, we offer monthly arrangements covering remote helpdesk, monitoring, and scheduled on-site visits — sized around what your business actually needs.

## Our principles

- **Practical over theoretical** — we focus on what your business needs to function reliably, not on selling complexity
- **Honest scoping** — we quote for what's needed, not what generates the largest invoice
- **Arabic and English** — all documentation, training, and communication delivered in both languages
- **On-site when it matters** — remote support where it works, on-site when it doesn't

## On regulatory topics

We stay informed on IT-related regulatory developments in KSA — including NCA ECC, PDPL, and SAMA requirements — and can discuss what they mean for your business in practical terms. We are not a licensed auditing or compliance certification body. For formal assessments or regulatory submissions, we recommend engaging a licensed specialist and are happy to assist with the preparation work around that process.
