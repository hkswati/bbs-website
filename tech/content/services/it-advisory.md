Many small and medium businesses reach a point where IT decisions matter — but don't yet need a full-time IT manager. We fill that gap with practical, vendor-neutral advice.

## What we can help with

- Reviewing your current IT setup and flagging what needs attention
- Helping you understand security frameworks relevant to your sector — what they require and how your current setup relates to them
- Drafting IT and security policies in Arabic and English
- Advising on technology decisions: hardware purchases, software choices, cloud vs on-premise
- Helping you organise and document your IT environment for due diligence or internal review purposes
- Vendor selection and evaluation support
- 12-month IT planning and priority setting

## How we work

We provide guidance and practical support — we are not a licensed auditing firm and do not issue compliance certifications. Our advisory work is designed to help you understand your environment and make better decisions, not to produce formal audit reports.

For businesses facing regulatory scrutiny (NCA ECC, PDPL, SAMA), we can help you understand what is being asked of you and assist in preparing documentation and processes — in coordination with any formally licensed advisors you may engage.

## Who this suits

- Owner-managed businesses with no internal IT function
- Companies preparing for enterprise client requirements or RFP processes
- Businesses that need someone to review a technology proposal before signing
- Teams that want help building internal IT policies without hiring a full-time person
