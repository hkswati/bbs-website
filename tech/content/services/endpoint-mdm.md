Unmanaged endpoints are one of the most common entry points for attackers. We deploy MDM solutions that give you visibility and control over every device in your organisation.

## What we deliver

- Device inventory and management via Fleet
- Security policy enforcement, remote wipe, and application management
- BYOD policy implementation and enforcement
- Secure remote access for staff via Headscale/Tailscale
- Microsoft Intune integration when required (client-licensed)
- Endpoint compliance reporting aligned to NCA ECC

## KSA relevance

Saudi organisations have large mobile workforces — often unmanaged. Fleet hosted inside KSA keeps device inventory data within the Kingdom. PDPL applies to employee personal data stored on managed devices, making local hosting a compliance requirement, not just a preference.
