We supply, install, and configure IP phone systems for offices of any size — from a handful of desk phones to multi-floor setups with call queues and auto-attendants.

## What we deliver

- Desk phone supply and installation (Yealink, Grandstream, and others)
- PBX setup using FreePBX, 3CX, or Asterisk — on-premise or hosted
- Internal extensions, call groups, and ring strategies
- Auto-attendant and IVR menu configuration (Arabic and English prompts)
- Voicemail setup and email-to-voicemail forwarding
- Call recording where required
- SIP trunk configuration with your chosen local carrier
- Integration with existing network infrastructure and switches
- Staff training and handover documentation

## On-premise or hosted

We can deploy your PBX on a server inside your office (keeping calls on your infrastructure) or recommend a hosted option depending on your size and preference. Either way, handsets and extensions are configured to work from day one.

## Adding to an existing setup

If you already have a phone system and need to expand, migrate, or troubleshoot, we handle that too.
