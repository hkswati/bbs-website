You cannot protect what you cannot see. Most Saudi SMEs with multiple branches have zero centralised asset visibility. We fix that with Snipe-IT — fully in your control.

## What we deliver

- Hardware inventory: laptops, desktops, servers, network equipment, printers
- Software licence tracking and compliance management
- Shadow IT discovery — identify devices and software that shouldn't be on your network
- Multi-branch asset tracking across all KSA locations
- Depreciation tracking and replacement planning
- Integration with helpdesk workflows

## Why this matters

Organisations that don't know what they own can't patch it, can't insure it, and can't comply with NCA ECC requirements around asset management. Snipe-IT gives your team a single source of truth — in Arabic and English.
