A well-designed network is the foundation of everything else. We design, install, and maintain LAN/WAN infrastructure for businesses operating from a single office or across multiple KSA locations.

## What we deliver

- Structured cabling (Cat5e/Cat6) — supply, installation, and testing
- Managed and unmanaged switch configuration (MikroTik, Ubiquiti, Cisco, TP-Link, and others)
- Wi-Fi access point design, installation, and coverage optimisation
- Router setup, internet failover, and bandwidth management
- Site-to-site VPN for multi-branch businesses (WireGuard, OpenVPN)
- Network monitoring via Zabbix or LibreNMS — fault alerting and performance dashboards
- VLAN configuration to separate staff, guest, and device traffic
- QoS (Quality of Service) for prioritising voice and video traffic

## Switches

We work with the full range — from basic unmanaged desktop switches for small offices to rack-mounted managed switches for larger environments requiring VLANs, port security, and SNMP monitoring.

## Multi-site

For businesses with branches across Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, or other KSA cities, we can design and implement a connected network that lets sites share resources securely. Self-hosted VPN options are available for clients who prefer to keep connectivity on their own infrastructure rather than relying on third-party cloud services.
