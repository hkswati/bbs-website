Shared passwords and unaudited privileged access are among the most exploited weaknesses in Saudi SME environments. We deploy a full PAM stack that gives your team complete visibility and control over privileged access.

## What we deliver

- Password vaulting via Passbolt — team password management, hosted on your infrastructure
- Secrets management via HashiCorp Vault for API keys, credentials, and certificates
- Privileged session recording via Teleport — full audit trail of who accessed what and when
- Just-in-time access provisioning — temporary elevated access with automatic revocation
- Shared credential elimination across IT and operations teams

## NCA ECC mapping

This service maps to NCA ECC Identity and Access Management (IAM) controls — specifically privileged account management, shared account elimination, and access logging requirements.
