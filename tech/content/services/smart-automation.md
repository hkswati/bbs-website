We design, install, and configure automation systems for homes and offices — covering lighting, climate, blinds, AV, and access control in a single integrated setup that can be controlled from a mobile app or a wall panel.

## Smart lighting

The most visible part of any automation setup. We configure:

- Lighting scenes (welcome, work, relax, presentation, night) per room or zone
- Dimmer control for individual circuits and groups
- Scheduled on/off and occupancy-based triggering
- Colour temperature adjustment for workspaces
- HDL Buspro and KNX wired bus systems for reliable, low-latency control
- Shelly and similar devices for retrofitting existing wiring without full rewiring

## Climate control

- Smart thermostat integration with split units and central HVAC
- Zone-based temperature scheduling — different settings per area and time of day
- Integration with lighting scenes (e.g. "away" mode adjusts both lighting and climate)
- Remote control and monitoring via app

## Motorised blinds and curtains

- Motorised roller blinds, curtain tracks, and venetian blinds
- Scheduled open/close based on time or sunlight levels
- Integration with lighting and climate scenes

## AV and media

- Centralised AV control — one interface for TV, projector, speakers, and streaming
- Multi-room audio zoning
- Meeting room setup: one-touch start for display, audio, and video conferencing
- Integration with room booking or occupancy systems where needed

## Smart access

- Smart lock installation and configuration (keypad, card, app, and biometric options)
- Door and gate automation for offices and compounds
- Integration with the broader automation system — doors that unlock when a scene activates, or lock when the building goes into "night" mode

## Control interfaces

- Mobile app control (iOS and Android) for the full system
- Physical wall panels and touchscreens
- Voice assistant integration where applicable
- Remote access for facility managers and building owners

## Our approach

We work with wired bus systems (HDL Buspro, KNX) for new builds and major fit-outs where reliability matters, and with Wi-Fi/cloud devices (Shelly, Tuya, Home Assistant) for retrofits and smaller budgets. Both approaches can be integrated into a single control layer.

All systems are configured, documented, and handed over with staff training. We stay available for scene adjustments, additions, and troubleshooting after delivery.
