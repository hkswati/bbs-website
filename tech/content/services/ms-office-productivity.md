We set up, migrate, and support the productivity and office software your team uses day-to-day — from email and documents through to collaboration tools and file storage.

## Microsoft 365 — the most common setup we handle

Microsoft 365 is the most widely used productivity platform for businesses in KSA. We handle the full setup:

- Tenant creation and custom domain configuration
- Business email on Exchange Online (your own domain, e.g. name@yourcompany.com.sa)
- Email migration from existing providers or on-premise Exchange
- Microsoft Teams — channels, permissions, guest access, and meetings
- SharePoint and OneDrive for file sharing and storage
- Licence management — right-sizing plans, adding and removing users
- MFA setup and basic security hardening
- Arabic-language configuration throughout
- Staff training and handover

## Other platforms we support

Not every business needs Microsoft 365. We also work with:

- **Google Workspace** — Gmail, Drive, Meet, and Docs for businesses that prefer the Google ecosystem
- **Standalone Microsoft Office** — installation, volume licensing, and activation on Windows and Mac
- **Zoho Workplace** — a cost-effective alternative with email, docs, and collaboration tools
- **LibreOffice** — for organisations that prefer open-source office software

## Business email

If you just need professional email on your own domain without the full productivity suite, we handle that too — whether through Microsoft, Google, Zoho, or a standalone mail server.

## Ongoing support

After setup we stay available for user additions, licence changes, troubleshooting, and questions. No hunting for a new vendor every time something needs adjusting.
