We handle fiber optic installations from splicing through to full commissioning — new builds, office fit-outs, backbone upgrades, and ISP last-mile connections.

## What we deliver

- Fusion splicing — single-mode and multimode fiber
- OTDR testing to verify splice quality, identify faults, and document link performance
- Patch panel and ODF termination
- Fiber tray organisation and labelling
- End-to-end link commissioning with full test documentation
- Fault finding and repair on existing fiber runs
- ISP last-mile fiber termination and handover
- Coordination with civil works teams for conduit runs

## Documentation provided

Every fiber job includes an OTDR trace report with event markers, splice loss values, and total link loss — useful for building records and future troubleshooting.

## Environments we work in

- Commercial office buildings and fit-outs
- Industrial and warehouse facilities
- Compound and campus environments
- Data rooms and server room backbone links
