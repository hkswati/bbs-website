DNS-level filtering blocks malicious domains before a connection is ever made — one of the highest-ROI security controls a business can implement.

## What we deliver

- Pi-hole or AdGuard Home deployment — self-hosted on your network
- Arabic-language phishing domain blocklists updated regularly
- Advertising and tracking domain blocking
- Content filtering aligned to KSA internet guidelines
- DNS query logging for threat investigation

## Why DNS security in KSA

Arabic-language phishing domains targeting businesses in KSA are growing rapidly — spoofed business portals, supplier impersonation sites, and financial institution lookalikes that standard Western blocklists don't cover. Our Arabic-focused blocklists are specifically curated for the local threat landscape.
