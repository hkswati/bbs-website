Business Email Compromise (BEC) is one of the fastest-growing cyber threats in Saudi Arabia, targeting trading companies, construction firms, and professional services. We harden your email environment to stop it.

## What we deliver

- SPF, DKIM, and DMARC configuration and testing
- Email filtering via Proxmox Mail Gateway
- BEC protection: display name spoofing detection, lookalike domain alerts
- Email archiving via MailArchiva
- Microsoft 365 tenant setup, hardening, and Arabic language configuration
- Conditional Access policies and MFA enforcement
- Anti-phishing policies and safe links configuration

## KSA relevance

Arabic-language BEC lures are increasingly sophisticated. Saudi trading and construction companies are disproportionately targeted — fake invoice fraud in particular. Our team understands Arabic social engineering patterns that generic Western security tools miss.
