You cannot respond to threats you haven't detected. Our SIEM service gives you enterprise-grade continuous monitoring, hosted inside KSA, with reports delivered in Arabic and English every month.

## What we deliver

- Wazuh SIEM/XDR deployment — hosted on a KSA-based server you control
- Log ingestion from firewalls, endpoints, servers, and applications
- Custom detection rules calibrated to the Saudi threat landscape (BEC, ZATCA phishing, credential attacks)
- Threat hunting via Velociraptor for incident investigation
- Arabic and English dashboards with drill-down capability
- Monthly threat report delivered by Day 5 — incidents detected, alerts fired, trends

## Compliance mapping

Wazuh maps to NCA ECC continuous monitoring controls, SAMA Cybersecurity Framework monitoring requirements, and supports PDPL incident detection obligations. All log data is hosted inside KSA.
