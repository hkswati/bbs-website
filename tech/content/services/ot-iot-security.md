Smart buildings, industrial control systems, and unmanaged IoT devices are among the least secured attack surfaces in KSA. Almost no one is securing them — we do.

## What we deliver

- Network segmentation between IT (office) and OT/industrial systems — physically isolated VLANs
- Rogue device detection using Wazuh — unauthorised devices on your network trigger alerts
- Wi-Fi penetration testing using Aircrack-ng and Kismet — identify rogue access points and weak configurations
- Printer and peripheral hardening — one of the most overlooked attack vectors in Saudi offices
- IoT device inventory and policy enforcement
- Smart building system security assessment

## Who this is for

- Commercial compounds and office towers with building management systems
- Manufacturing and light industrial facilities
- Hospitals and clinics with connected medical devices
- Schools and universities with IoT infrastructure
- Any business where someone asked "can we put this on the network?" and the answer was yes without a security review
