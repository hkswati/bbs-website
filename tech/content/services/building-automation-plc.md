Small and medium businesses running equipment, production lines, or facilities need their automation to work reliably — and increasingly, to be visible remotely. We commission new PLC systems, modify existing ones, deploy IoT sensors, and connect it all to monitoring that gives your team actionable visibility.

## PLC commissioning

- New PLC installations for light manufacturing, utilities, and facilities — Siemens S7-1200/S7-1500, Schneider Modicon M221/M241, Allen Bradley Micro820/Micro850
- Programming in Ladder Logic, Function Block Diagram (FBD), and Structured Text
- HMI panel setup and configuration
- Modifications and upgrades to existing PLC installations
- Fault diagnosis on legacy systems — including undocumented or poorly documented installs
- I/O mapping, wiring review, and full documentation handover: programme comments, I/O lists, and wiring schedules

## IoT sensor deployment

- Temperature, humidity, flow, pressure, and vibration sensors — wired and wireless
- Industrial IoT gateways to connect legacy equipment to your network without replacing it
- Energy monitoring per machine or per circuit — visibility into consumption and anomalies
- Alert configuration: email or SMS notification when parameters go outside defined thresholds
- Data logging for maintenance planning, fault history, and equipment performance review

## Remote monitoring

- Dashboards giving operators clear visibility over equipment and process status
- Remote access to PLCs for diagnostics and parameter adjustments without on-site visits
- Integration with existing IT infrastructure where required — connecting operational data to business systems

## Environments

- Light manufacturing and food production
- Warehouses, cold storage, and logistics facilities
- Water, HVAC, and utilities management
- Any SMB facility with equipment that needs to run reliably to a schedule

If your automation requirement doesn't fit neatly into one of these categories, get in touch and we'll tell you whether we can help.
