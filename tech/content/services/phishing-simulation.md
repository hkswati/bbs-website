Generic Western phishing templates fail in Saudi Arabia. Our simulations use locally relevant lures — business portal notifications, financial institution alerts, local service provider messages, and HR system notices — in Arabic.

## What we deliver

- Phishing simulation campaigns using GoPhish
- Arabic-language lure templates: business portals, financial institutions, HR and payroll systems, local service providers
- Click-rate reporting by department, seniority, and device type
- Bilingual staff awareness training (Arabic + English)
- Repeat campaigns to measure improvement over time
- Quarterly campaigns available in managed plans

## Why Arabic campaigns matter

A phishing email in English is easier for Saudi staff to spot as suspicious. Arabic-language lures using locally familiar branding bypass that natural filter. Click rates on Arabic, locally contextualised lures are significantly higher than generic templates — which means the training value is also significantly higher.
